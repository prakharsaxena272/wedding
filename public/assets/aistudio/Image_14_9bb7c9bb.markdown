## Brand & Style

The brand personality channels timeless romance, curated bespoke hospitality, and modern couture wedding editorial. Crafted for discerning couples, elite wedding concierges, and premier bridal ateliers, the visual identity balances poetic warmth with uncompromising clarity.

The aesthetic philosophy is an elevated convergence of **Warm Minimalist Editorial** and **Delicate Tactile Classicism**:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Warm ivory canvases enriched by deep antique gold foils, muted bronze accents, and cool periwinkle highlights.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast display serifs paired with whisper-quiet, spacious grotesque typography.
- **Subtle Tactility:** Filigree-weight borders, micro-embossing cues, frosted pearlescent glass sheets, and organic pill capsules that echo hand-pressed stationery and wax seal accents.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, tranquil, luxurious, and celebratory without visual clutter or kitsch.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated strictly around natural bridal textiles, botanical garlands, and hand-gilded metallics:

- **Primary (`#A68503` — Antique Gold):** Reserved for celebratory focal points, luxury badges, verified vendor crests, active navigation indicators, and primary call-to-action treatments. Never over-saturated; rendered with warm metallic balance.
- **Secondary (`#877645` — Muted Bronze):** Used for romantic highlights, RSVP milestones, celebratory alerts, and tender interactive selections.
- **Tertiary (`#97B0FF` — Soft Periwinkle):** Evokes crisp evening skies, subtle accent states, verified schedule states, confirmed bookings, and modern botanical contrasts.
- **Neutral (`#7C766B` — Warm Taupe Bronze):** Used for primary typography, refined vector filigree, and structural boundaries. Avoids stark pitch-black (`#000000`) in favor of warm, legible, sophisticated ink tones.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFDF9`):** Pristine bridal parchment base.
- **Warm Surface / Card Canvas (`#FAF6F0`):** Soft clotted cream used for cards, bottom sheets, and elevated overlays.
- **Muted Surface / Dividers (`#F3ECE2`):** Subtle tone for structural lines, inactive field containers, and nested sub-panels.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses high-fashion stationery meeting seamless digital utility:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines):** Classical, transitional serif with deliberate stroke contrast. Evokes French letterpress invitations, gilded event programs, and legacy titles. Used for all primary page titles, vendor hero names, and ceremonial section greetings.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Microcopy):** Soft, humanist geometric sans-serif that balances the classicism of the serif with crisp legibility on small retina screens.
- **`label-caps` Rule:** Always styled in full uppercase with generous tracking (`0.12em`) to introduce couture stationery badges, categorization headers, and status markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, spacious fluid grid system tailored for both immersive visual browsing and quick tactical wedding management:

- **Mobile Viewports (<768px):** Single-column stacked flow using `margin` of `1.25rem` and `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Tap targets adhere to a strict minimum 44px height. Bottom sheets and sticky pill footers keep vendor communication accessible.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid. Split hero views allow photo galleries to sit alongside event itinerary cards.
- **Desktop Viewports (>1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px`. Generous outer canvas margins (`3rem`) preserve negative space reminiscent of an open wedding album.

Spacing scale strictly defines layout separation:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between icons and adjacent text labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Spacing inside compact tags, pill badges, and input prefixes.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard padding for lists, cards, and interactive button groups.
- `space-lg` (24px): Generous card padding and spacing between stacked input groups.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section divisions and milestone transitions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft diffusion and layered stationery tones, rejecting harsh drop shadows:

- **Warm Multi-Tiers:** Depth is established primarily through shifting surfaces—from the primary canvas (`#FFFDF9`) to elevated modular cards (`#FAF6F0`), edged with fine micro-borders (`#EFE5D8`).
- **Gilded Luster (Gold Foil Ambient Shadow):** Top-tier cards and floating action modules use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted in warm umber and gold: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(166, 133, 3, 0.10), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(124, 118, 107, 0.04)`.
- **Frosted Veil (Glassmorphism):** Floating navigation bars, sticky guest filters, and dialog backdrops employ a delicate frosted glass treatment: `background: rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a `1px` subtle rim border of `rgba(166, 133, 3, 0.22)`.
- **Delicate Floral Filigree Outlines:** Secondary cards incorporate an inset or dual micro-border (`1px solid #F1E8DC` with an inner `1px solid rgba(135, 118, 69, 0.25)`), evoking blind-debossed paper stock.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a pronounced pill-leaning roundedness configuration (`level 3` - 1rem base radius) complemented by fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) for contextual badges and interactive controls:

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Panels, Inputs, Modals):** Base radius of `1rem` (16px), scaling to `1.5rem` (24px) on hero feature containers (`rounded-lg`) and `2rem` (32px) on full-screen drawers (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Philosophy (Buttons, Chips, Status Tags, Tabs):** Interactive tags and contextual buttons utilize full pill radii (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create an inviting, tactile contrast against rectangular stationery cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Foil Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with antique gold (`#A68503`), displaying light ink text (`#FFFFFF`) in `title-sm`. Elevated with a subtle warm glow. Hover state deepens to `#8c7002`.
- **Romantic Secondary:** Pill-shaped, semi-transparent bronze tint (`rgba(135, 118, 69, 0.15)`), bordered with a delicate bronze rim (`1px solid #877645`), text rendered in espresso.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Borderless, using `title-sm` with an animated gold underline on hover and 44px minimum hit bounds.

### Vendor Status & Category Pill Tags
- **Confirmed / Booked:** Soft periwinkle container (`rgba(151, 176, 255, 0.15)`), periwinkle text (`#3c5ec4`), `label-caps` typography, framed with a `1px` periwinkle rim.
- **In Inquiry / Pending:** Bronze container (`rgba(135, 118, 69, 0.18)`), deep bronze text (`#635531`), `label-caps`.
- **Signature / Luxury Badge:** Antique gold container (`rgba(166, 133, 3, 0.14)`), primary gold text (`#7a6102`), accompanied by a delicate 12px starburst or botanical vector emblem.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields:** Surface tone `#FAF6F0`, border `1px solid #E8DEC8`. Focused state reveals a warm gold rim (`#A68503`) and a subtle gold ambient halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(166, 133, 3, 0.15)`). Text in warm neutral with placeholder in muted warm umber (`#A89F91`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic corners (`rounded-sm` for checkbox, full pill for radio), borders colored in `#D6CAB8`. When checked, filled with antique gold `#A68503` displaying an ivory checkmark.

### Cards & Stationery Panels
- Constructed using warm ivory canvas (`#FAF6F0`), surrounded by an ultra-thin botanical border (`1px solid #EFE4D6`).
- **Special Bridal Feature Card:** Incorporates double-border framing: an outer `1px solid #E6DAC8` and an inset margin of `4px` displaying a hairline border in soft bronze (`rgba(135, 118, 69, 0.4)`), mimicking bespoke envelope lining.

### Lists & Timelines
- Separated by soft warm dividers (`#F3ECE2`). Timeline nodes for the wedding day run sheet are depicted as delicate gold ring markers linked via a 1.5px vertical periwinkle line.
